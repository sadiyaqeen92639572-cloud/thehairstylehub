Short Curly Hairstyle Tutorial

By Jasmine Carter · Published 2026-07-19 · Easy difficulty · 20 min

Short curly hair behaves differently from long curly hair — with less weight to pull the curl down, the same amount of product that looks perfect on longer hair can leave short curls looking heavy or crunchy instead of soft.

The technique that actually works for shorter lengths leans on applying product to soaking-wet hair and letting gravity and drying time do the rest, rather than trying to compensate with more gel.

Resist the urge to touch the hair while it dries — every time fingers move through a curl before it sets fully, that curl loses definition and picks up frizz.

Short Curly Hairstyle Tutorial

Difficulty: Easy Time to style: 20 min Hair type: curly, wavy Hair length: short

What You'll Need

  • Wide-tooth comb
  • Microfiber towel or old t-shirt
  • Diffuser attachment (optional)
  • Lightweight leave-in conditioner
  • Curl cream or gel

Steps

  1. 1. Detangle in the shower

    Apply conditioner and detangle with a wide-tooth comb while the hair is soaked and slippery — detangling dry short curls causes far more breakage and frizz.

  2. 2. Scrunch out water

    Flip the hair forward and scrunch with a microfiber towel or cotton t-shirt to remove excess water without roughing up the cuticle the way a regular towel does.

  3. 3. Apply product to soaking-wet hair

    Rake a small amount of leave-in conditioner through, then scrunch in a curl cream or gel while the hair is still dripping — product applied to already-drying hair sits on top instead of coating each strand.

  4. 4. Dry without disturbing the curls

    Air dry, or diffuse on low heat with the diffuser cupped close to the scalp, lifting sections gently rather than moving the dryer back and forth.

  5. 5. Break the cast gently

    Once fully dry, scrunch out the crunchy gel cast with clean, dry hands to release soft, separated curls.

Tips & Common Questions

Why do short curls frizz more than long curls?

Shorter curls have less weight pulling them down, so any excess frizz or halo effect is more visible — the fix is less about more product and more about applying it evenly to soaking-wet hair.

Is a diffuser worth it for short curly hair?

It speeds up drying time significantly and helps curls clump instead of frizzing loose, though air-drying gives the softest result if time allows.

How often should short curly hair actually be washed?

Every 2-4 days is typical for most curl patterns — over-washing strips the natural oils curls rely on for definition, while refreshing with water between washes keeps curls looking fresh.
