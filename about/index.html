About The Hairstyle Hub

The Hairstyle Hub is written by Jasmine Carter, a licensed cosmetologist who has worked behind the chair for over a decade, most recently at a braid- and natural-hair-focused salon. Every tutorial here — braids, curly hair, updos, kids hair, men's cuts or wig installs — is written the way she'd actually walk a client or a friend through it in the chair, not as a generic style description copied from a photo.

How tutorials are written

Each tutorial covers the real technique behind a style — sectioning, tension, product order, and the specific mistakes that make a style fall apart early — rather than just describing what the finished look is supposed to look like. Realistic time estimates and difficulty ratings are based on how long a style actually takes a reasonably practiced person to do, not a professional working at salon speed.
