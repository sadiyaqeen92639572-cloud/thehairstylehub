Toddler Girl Hairstyle Tutorial

By Jasmine Carter · Published 2026-07-19 · Easy difficulty · 5 min

Toddler hair styling has a lower bar than it looks like online — the goal isn't an elaborate braid, it's getting hair out of the face in under five minutes without a meltdown.

Low, loose pigtail puffs check both boxes: they take almost no technique, and the reduced tension compared to a high ponytail is genuinely more comfortable on a small, sensitive scalp.

Jump to Tutorial ↓

Brush from the ends upward, never starting at the root and pulling down through knots — this single change in brushing direction removes most of the pain a toddler associates with getting their hair done.

Toddler Girl Hairstyle Tutorial

Difficulty: Easy Time to style: 5 min Hair type: fine, wavy, curly Hair length: short-to-medium

What You'll Need

  • Soft-bristle brush
  • Snag-free elastics
  • Leave-in detangling spray

Steps

  1. 1. Distract before starting

    Sit the toddler somewhere with a screen or toy in hand — most of the difficulty with toddler hair comes from squirming, not the styling itself.

  2. 2. Spray and gently brush

    Mist a light detangling spray through the hair and use a soft-bristle brush in short, gentle strokes starting from the ends and working up, never pulling from the root down.

  3. 3. Part down the middle

    Use fingers rather than a comb to part the hair evenly down the middle — a comb part can feel sharp on a sensitive toddler scalp.

  4. 4. Gather each side loosely

    Gather each side into a small, low puff or pigtail near the ear rather than high on the crown, using minimal tension.

  5. 5. Secure without snagging

    Wrap a snag-free, soft elastic around each section just enough times to hold — over-tightening is the main cause of toddler hairstyle meltdowns.

Tips & Common Questions

Why does my toddler cry every time we do her hair?

It's almost always tension, not the styling itself — starting the brush from the ends, using loose low pigtails instead of tight high ones, and snag-free elastics solve most of it.

How short can hair be and still work for pigtail puffs?

As little as an inch or two is enough for small puffs, since the goal with a toddler style is just to gather it, not to make a long tail — even short baby hair usually gathers fine.

Is any product necessary for toddler hair?

A light leave-in or detangling spray helps significantly with the brushing step, but heavier gels and creams generally aren't needed for hair this fine and short.
